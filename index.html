Grownups' Reading Notes

These original stories are designed around short vowels, simple consonant blends and the sight words the, to, are. They are an approximate fit for Lesson 38, not a verified Reading.com scope-and-sequence match.

Important: Some words in these early drafts may be unfamiliar or not yet taught. In Kit’s book, page 8 includes dry, which should be parent-assisted for now. Preview each story before independent reading, and read or replace any words outside Zara's current phonics inventory.
